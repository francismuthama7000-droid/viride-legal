Privacy Policy

Viride helps passengers find verified boda-boda (motorbike) riders organised in local stages. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. Last updated October 2026.

Who we are

Viride operates this app. For any privacy question or request, contact us at viridesupport@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account details: your name, phone number, email, and profile photo. Identity and documents: chairmen provide a National ID number; riders provide a motorbike number plate plus an NTSA driving licence and insurance, which their stage chairman reviews. Location: your device location while you request or carry out a trip, to match you with nearby riders and show pickup and drop-off. Device: a notification token so we can alert you about ride requests and trip updates. Activity: your trips and ratings. Reports: if you report someone you rode with, or someone reports you, the reason and any details written.

Why we use it

To provide and run the service (matching passengers with nearby verified riders and managing trips); to keep the community safe through stage verification; to send you ride and trip notifications; and to meet legal and safety obligations. We rely on your consent and on what is necessary to provide a service you asked for.

Who we share it with

Your profile photo is visible only to you, to people you share a trip with, and, for riders, to their stage chairman. Your stage chairman can view a rider's verification documents in order to approve them, and sees reports about riders in their stage, but never who made a report. Viride reviews every report. We use trusted providers to run the app: Supabase (database and file hosting, in the UK/EU region) and Google (sign-in, maps, place search, and push notifications through Firebase). We never sell your personal data.

Where your data is stored

Your data is hosted in the UK/EU region. Because that is outside Kenya, this is a cross-border transfer; we keep it protected and only use providers with strong security.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. If you deactivate, you can reactivate within 30 days by signing in; after that the account is closed. See Delete your account.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 you can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, and object to certain uses. Use the Profile screen or contact us. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

Security

Verification documents are kept in private storage; access is restricted so only you and your stage chairman can view a rider's documents. Connections are encrypted.

Children

Viride is for adults (18+) and is not intended for children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will note the date above.

This is a plain-language policy for the current build. The final wording and ODPC registration are confirmed with a lawyer before public launch.
