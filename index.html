KUVETRA

Boda boda rides you can trust.

Kuvetra connects passengers with boda boda riders who are verified by the chairman of their own stage. Built in Kenya, starting in Machakos.

Now in closed testing. The Google Play link will appear here at launch.

How it works

Passengers

  • Request a ride from where you are to where you are going.
  • The nearest available verified rider gets your request first.
  • See your rider's name, photo and plate, and follow the trip on the map.

Riders

  • Join your stage and get verified by your stage chairman.
  • Go online and receive ride requests near you.
  • Navigate to the pickup and the destination.

Stage chairmen

  • Register your stage and check each rider's documents.
  • Approve the riders you know.
  • See your riders, their ratings and reports.

Safety first

Every rider is checked by their own stage chairman before taking rides. Passengers can rate riders, report a problem, and reach 999 or 112 from the trip screen. Emergency contacts you save stay on your phone only.
