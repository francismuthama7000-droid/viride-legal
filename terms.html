What Viride is

Viride is a technology platform that connects passengers with independent boda-boda (motorbike) riders organised into local stages, each led by a chairman. Viride is not a transport company and does not employ riders; riders operate independently.

Who can use it

You must be at least 18 and provide accurate information. You are responsible for activity on your account. You sign in with your Google account, so keep that account and your phone secure.

For riders

Riders must be verified by their stage chairman and must hold a valid driving licence and insurance that covers carrying passengers, obey all traffic laws, and ride safely, including wearing a helmet and carrying one for the passenger. An NTSA PSV badge is required, with a 45-day grace period from approval to obtain it. Submitting false documents can lead to removal. Riders are also bound by the Rider Agreement.

Fares

Agree the fare with the rider before the trip begins, and pay the rider directly in cash or to their own M-Pesa. Viride never holds, collects or touches fare money.

Night fee for passengers

Trips that start between 6am and 6pm are free for passengers. For each trip that starts between 6pm and 6am, Viride charges the passenger a night fee, separate from the fare paid to the rider: KES 5 when the fare is below KES 150, and KES 10 when the fare is KES 150 or more.

You pay the night fee from a top-up balance you load yourself through M-Pesa. That balance can only ever be used to pay Viride’s night fee. It cannot be withdrawn as cash, cannot be refunded, and cannot be sent to another user.

Viride does not charge the night fee yet. We will give at least 14 days’ notice in the app before charging starts. Riders pay a commission set out in the Rider Agreement.

Safety and conduct

Treat other users with respect. Do not use Viride for anything illegal or unsafe. Passengers and riders rate each other after a trip; repeated low ratings or rule-breaking can lead a chairman to suspend a rider.

Stages and governance

Stages are self-governed by chairmen, who verify riders and may suspend or reinstate them. A rider belongs to one stage and can only move with the agreement of both chairmen.

Your responsibility and risk

Travel by motorbike carries risk and you take part voluntarily. Because riders are independent, Viride is not responsible for their conduct or for incidents during a trip, to the maximum extent allowed by law. Always confirm the rider and number plate match the app before you ride.
